# RAG Reading Group — Literature Scan Digest

## Themes: efficiency, reasoning, multimodality, safety

### Efficiency
- **arXiv:2601.07711** "Is Agentic RAG worth it? An experimental comparison of RAG approaches" (posted 2026-01-12) — Must-read. Experimental comparison of "Enhanced" vs "Agentic" RAG across scenarios, guidance on selecting the best RAG design balancing performance and cost.

### Reasoning
- **arXiv:2507.09477** "Towards Agentic RAG with Deep Reasoning: A Survey of RAG-Reasoning Systems in LLMs" (posted 2025-07-13) — Must-read. Survey on RAG-Reasoning systems, mapping reasoning-enhanced and synergized retrieval-and-reasoning frameworks.
- **arXiv:2601.08699** "Eliciting Sophisticated Agentic RAG Skills via Automated Data Synthesis" (posted 2026-01-13) — Worth skimming. Explores how automated data synthesis can elicit advanced agentic RAG skills for complex problem-solving.

### Multimodality
- **arXiv:2505.11180** "mmRAG: A Modular Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation over Text, Tables, and Knowledge Graphs" (posted 2025-05-16) — Must-read. Modular benchmark evaluating multi-modal RAG across text, tables, and knowledge graphs.

### Safety
- **arXiv:2407.11005** "RAGBench: Explainable Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems" (posted 2024-06-25) — Must-read. Large-scale RAG benchmark dataset with traceable TRACe evaluation metrics for explainable, industry-specific evaluation.

## Recent developments
The wider web highlights RAG maturing into agentic, multi-step retrieval and reasoning systems. Notable chatter: experimental trade-offs in agentic RAG (arXiv:2601.07711), the deep-reasoning survey (arXiv:2507.09477), and emerging modular multi-modal benchmarks (arXiv:2505.11180).
